    *Final check on the requirement:* "Unary minus/plus binds weaker than `^` (`-2^2` = -4)".
    Wait, my grammar has `Unary` calling `Power`.
    In recursive descent, the function called *later* has *higher* precedence.
    If `Unary` calls `Power`, then `Power` is higher precedence.
    `Unary` is `-` then `Power(2^2)`. So `- (2^2) = -4`.

    *What about `++2`?*
